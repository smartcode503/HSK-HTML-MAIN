# Paper map

HSK 2.0 reading only. The builder checks the span in `READING` inside `scripts/build_notes.py`. Change both together.

The JSON field is `"part": 1` (or 2, 3, 4). The builder prints the part line. Group by the passage, word bank, or sentence bank printed on this paper. The groups named for H31008, H41009, H51553C, and H61001 are what those papers do. Another paper of the same level can break a part at different question numbers.

## HSK 3 · questions 41–70

Three parts. Main vocabulary table: HSK 3. Higher table: HSK 4, 5, and 6. Leave out HSK 1 and 2.

Part 1, questions 41–50. Two units, 41–45 and 46–50, unless this paper is printed as one block. Each unit matches five numbered lines to six lettered sentences. One sentence is the printed example. Each question’s wrong rows are the other five sentences, and the example row is labeled `Does not answer`.

Part 2, questions 51–60. Two units, 51–55 and 56–60. Six words, one already used by the example, five blanks. Each question’s wrong rows are the other five words. The example word is `Does not answer`. For a word that fills another blank, say which blank.

Part 3, questions 61–70. One short passage and one question is one unit. Wrong rows are the other printed choices.

## HSK 4 · questions 46–85

Three parts. Main table: HSK 4. Higher table: HSK 5 and 6. Leave out HSK 1–3.

Part 1, questions 46–55. Two word-bank units, 46–50 and 51–55. Same wrong-row rule as HSK 3 part 2.

Part 2, questions 56–65. Each trio of sentences is one unit and one question. Wrong rows are exactly two orders a reader is likely to try, not the other five permutations. Name the link that breaks (`因此`, `否则`, `并且`, `那样`, `甚至`, `另外`).

Part 3, questions 66–85. One passage is one unit. On H41009 the paired passages are 80–81, 82–83, and 84–85, and 66–79 are one question each. Follow this paper’s pairing. Wrong rows are the other printed choices.

## HSK 5 · questions 46–90

Three parts. Main table: HSK 4 and HSK 5. Higher table: HSK 6. Leave out HSK 1–3.

Part 1, questions 46–60. One gap-fill passage is one unit. H51553C splits them 46–48, 49–52, 53–56, 57–60. Wrong rows are the other options for that blank.

Part 2, questions 61–70. One short text and one question is one unit. The task is to choose the statement that agrees with the passage. Wrong rows are the other three statements.

Part 3, questions 71–90. One article is one unit. H51553C puts four questions on each of five articles. Wrong rows are the other printed choices.

## HSK 6 · questions 51–100

Four parts. The model is `H61001-阅读解析.docx` (27 units). Main table: HSK 5 and HSK 6. There is no higher HSK band. The second table is the off-list table, headed `Also in this passage  ·  Above the HSK 6 list`. Leave out HSK 1–4. Put a word there only when the question depends on it.

Part 1, questions 51–60. Each item is four sentences; choose the one with a grammar problem (语病). One item is one unit. The why-paragraph names the error: redundant pattern, double negative, missing word, mixed pattern, subject mismatch, word order, or the wrong word. Wrong rows are the other three sentences, labeled `Looks odd`, each with why that sentence is acceptable.

Part 2, questions 61–70. On H61001 each number is its own item: several blanks, and four word groups. One item is one unit. When a paper prints one shared passage for several numbers, that passage is one unit. Wrong rows are the other groups. Say which blank the group fails.

Part 3, questions 71–80. Choose a sentence for each blank. H61001 groups 71–75 (the fish and the earth’s pull) and 76–80 (paper and printing). One passage is one unit. Wrong rows are the other sentences in that bank. Say which blank a sentence fills, or why it cannot fill this blank. Five options, so four wrong rows.

Part 4, questions 81–100. One article is one unit, four questions each. H61001 is 81–84, 85–88, 89–92, 93–96, and 97–100. Wrong rows are the other printed choices. Follow this paper’s grouping when it differs.

## What is the same on every level

Reading only. English notes. Chinese stays in the word, the answer line, and the quotation. One unit is one shared passage, word bank, or sentence bank, or one item when the paper prints it alone. The first vocabulary table is the new band for that level. Words from earlier levels stay out. A second table appears only when the unit has words above that band, or, on HSK 6, words the question needs that are not on the 2012 list. Every wrong choice the paper map requires gets a row. The answer letter is not the opening of the why-paragraph. Listening and writing stay out of the file.
